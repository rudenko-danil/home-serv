Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

This policy explains what information Home-Serv Appliance Repair ("we", "us") collects when you visit this website or book a service, and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Booking details. When you book online, you enter your name, phone number, email, service address, the appliance and a description of the problem. Online booking is handled by Zenbooker, our scheduling provider.
  • Calls, texts and emails. If you contact us, we keep your phone number or email and the details you share so we can schedule and complete the service.
  • Website usage data. Like most websites, we and our providers collect technical data such as your IP address, browser type, pages visited, the page you came from and ad click identifiers (for example, gclid).

Cookies and advertising

We use Google Ads and the Google tag to measure which ads lead to bookings and calls. These tools use cookies and similar technologies. Zenbooker may also use cookies to record how you found us when you book. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. You can control personalized Google ads at adssettings.google.com. Learn how Google uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

How we use your information

  • To schedule appointments, perform repairs and contact you about your service.
  • To provide estimates, invoices and warranty service.
  • To measure and improve our website and advertising.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run our business, such as Zenbooker (scheduling) and Google (advertising and measurement), or when required by law.

Retention

We keep booking and service records as long as needed to provide the service, honor warranties and meet legal and tax requirements.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information by emailing support@home-serv.com. We will respond within a reasonable time.

Children

This website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
