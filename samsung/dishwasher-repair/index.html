Common Samsung dishwasher problems we fix

Our technicians repair these Samsung dishwasher issues every week.

  • Dishwasher not drainingStanding water left at the bottom after the cycle.
  • Dishwasher not cleaning dishesFood residue, film or spots after a full wash.
  • Dishwasher leakingWater on the floor or seeping from under the door.
  • Dishwasher won't startLights on but nothing runs, or no power at all.
  • Dishwasher not filling with waterYou hear it run but there's no water in the tub.
  • Dishwasher not drying dishesDishes come out wet at the end of the cycle.
  • Dishwasher making noiseGrinding, humming or buzzing during the wash.
  • Dishwasher door won't latchThe door won't close or won't stay shut.

Parts we replace: drain pump, wash motor, water inlet valve, heating element, door latch, door gasket, float switch and control board.
