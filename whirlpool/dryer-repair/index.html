Common Whirlpool dryer problems we fix

Our technicians repair these Whirlpool dryer issues every week.

  • Dryer not heatingThe drum turns but clothes stay cold and damp.
  • Dryer takes too long to dryClothes need two or more cycles to get dry.
  • Dryer won't startNothing happens, or it hums without tumbling.
  • Dryer drum not turningThe motor runs but the drum stays still.
  • Dryer making loud noiseSqueaking, thumping, scraping or grinding.
  • Dryer overheating or shutting offHot to the touch or stops in the middle of a cycle.
  • Burning smell from the dryerStop using it until it has been checked.
  • Dryer door won't close or latchA broken latch or door switch stops the cycle.

Parts we replace: drive belt, heating element, thermal fuse, thermostats, gas igniter, gas valve coils, drum rollers and idler pulley.

Slow drying? Check the vent too

A clogged or crushed dryer vent is a common reason a dryer overheats or takes too long. We clean dryer vents as well.

  • Clothes still damp after a full cycle
  • Dryer or laundry room gets very hot
  • Lint collects around the door or the outside vent flap
